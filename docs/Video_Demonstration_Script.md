### Tips for Recording
1. **Screen Resolution:** Record in 1080p (1920x1080) in full-screen browser mode (press F11).
2. **Audio Quality:** Use a USB headset or external microphone in a quiet room.
3. **Cursor Visibility:** Enable mouse highlighting or smooth pointer motion.
4. **Interaction:** Click on filters and pre-built SQL buttons during the recording to demonstrate full interactivity!
