How to Connect Tableau to the Project Data Source
Step 1: Open Data Source in Tableau
  1. Launch Tableau Desktop or Tableau Public application.
  2. Under Connect, choose Text File.
  3. Select data/tableau/ev_market_tableau_ready.csv.
  4. Alternatively, connect via SQLite ODBC to data/database/ev_market.db and pick vw_ev_master_analytics.
Step 2: Build Visualizations & Publish
  1. Create the 12 unique visualizations outlined in the Design Guide.
  2. Assemble the 3 dashboards: Executive (Emily), BI Intel (David), and Clean Mobility (Dr. Sarah).
  3. Create the 5-Scene Story walkthrough.
  4. Click Server -> Tableau Public -> Save to Tableau Public and paste the link into the URL bar above!
Tableau Calculated Fields & LOD Expressions Reference
Calculated Field Tableau Formula Syntax Analytical Purpose
Energy Efficiency (km/kWh) [Driving Range Km] / [Battery Capacity Kwh] Evaluates real-world battery energy conversion efficiency.
Cost per KM of Range [Price Usd] / [Driving Range Km] Capital cost per kilometer of vehicle range.
Category Sales Share (LOD) {FIXED [Year], [Vehicle Category]: SUM([Annual Sales Units])} / {FIXED [Year]: SUM([Annual Sales Units])} LOD formula for exact category market share unaffected by external filters.
Charging Recovery Speed ([Driving Range Km] * 0.8) / [Fast Charge Time Min] Range replenishment rate in km added per minute of DC fast-charging.
CO2 Abatement Intensity ([Co2 Saved Per Vehicle Annual Mt] * 1000) / [Battery Carbon Footprint Kg] Lifecycle sustainability index comparing emissions avoided to battery manufacturing footprint.
