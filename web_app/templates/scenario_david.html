Competitive Intelligence Advisory for David Chen
1. The Duopoly Battle (Tesla vs BYD) BYD's vertical battery integration (Blade LFP) gives it a 15–22% cost-per-km advantage in sub-$35K vehicles. Tesla maintains premium software margin and superior DC fast-charging network moat in Western markets.
2. Supply Chain Nearshoring China represents over 52% of global EV assembly and battery cell production. However, US (Inflation Reduction Act) and European gigafactories in Germany and Hungary are capturing expanding local market share.
3. Strategic Market Entry Guidance New entrants must avoid fighting established pureplays head-on in mid-size sedans. High-potential entry wedges exist in specialized fleet commercial vehicles, electric commercial buses, and purpose-built urban micro-EVs.
