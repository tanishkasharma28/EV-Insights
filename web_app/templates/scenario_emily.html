Executive Takeaways for Emily Johnson
1. SUV & Crossover Primacy Electric SUVs continue to generate over 48% of total industry revenue. Expanding multi-trim SUV platforms with both budget LFP and extended-range NMC options offers the strongest risk-adjusted ROI.
2. Price Elasticity & "Sweet Spot" The quadrant analysis shows highest volume velocity in the $32,000–$48,000 range offering 400–500 km of driving range. Models priced above $75K face tapering demand unless accompanied by superior fast-charging.
3. Two-Wheeler Emerging Volume Electric two-wheelers in APAC and Latin America drive massive unit volume (20M+ units). While revenue per unit is lower, brand loyalty and fleet software services present an untapped monetization stream.
