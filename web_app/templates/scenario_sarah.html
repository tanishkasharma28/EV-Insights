Clean Mobility Research Findings for Dr. Sarah Williams
1. The Cobalt-Free Paradigm (LFP Dominance) Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) has expanded from 15% share in 2020 to over 40% in 2025. With cycle lives surpassing 3,000 cycles and high thermal stability, LFP significantly mitigates supply chain risk and fire hazards.
2. Solid-State & Sodium-Ion Horizon Sodium-Ion represents a breakthrough for affordable urban two-wheelers and energy storage. Early solid-state pilots (2024–2025) demonstrate energy densities >350 Wh/kg, paving the way for 750+ km highway ranges.
3. Decarbonization Net Positive Despite higher upstream battery cell manufacturing carbon footprints (65–90 kg CO2/kWh), vehicles offset their embedded footprint within 1.2 to 1.8 years of average driving when powered by mixed renewable grids.
